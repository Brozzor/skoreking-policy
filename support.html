Frequently asked questions

How do I start a new game? +

From the home screen, tap New Game, add your players by name, choose how many rounds you want to play, and start scoring. SkoreKing walks you through bids and tricks round by round.

How is the score calculated? +

SkoreKing follows the official Skull King rules:

  • Bid met exactly — 20 points per trick won.
  • Bid missed — you lose 10 points for each trick you were over or under.
  • Zero bid met — 10 points × the round number.
  • Bonuses — capturing the Skull King, pirates, mermaids, and 14s add extra points on top.

You can review the breakdown for each round on the scoreboard.

Can I use the app offline? +

Yes. SkoreKing works fully offline — no internet connection, account, or sign-in is ever required. Everything runs on your device.

Where is my game data stored? +

All of your games, scores, statistics, and settings are saved locally on your device only. Nothing is uploaded to a server. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.

I made a scoring mistake — can I fix it? +

Absolutely. You can go back to any previous round during a game and correct the bids, tricks won, or bonuses. The totals recalculate automatically.

How do I delete a game or clear my history? +

Delete individual games from the History screen, or use Clear history in Settings to remove all saved games and statistics at once. Uninstalling the app erases everything stored on your device.

Does SkoreKing support custom rules and bonuses? +

Yes — open Settings to adjust the rules, toggle optional bonuses, change the theme and language, and turn haptic feedback on or off to match how your group plays.

The app isn't working as expected. What should I do? +

Try these steps first:

  • Make sure you're on the latest version from the App Store / Google Play.
  • Fully close the app and reopen it.
  • Restart your device.

Still stuck? Email us at romain@buisson.us with your device model, OS version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots help a lot.
